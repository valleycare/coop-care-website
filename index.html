What we help with

Support shaped around the person, not the rota

Some people need a hand with shopping. Others need daily personal care, or support living with dementia or Parkinson's. We work on a "doing with, not doing to" basis, so independence lasts longer.

  • Personal care

    Washing, bathing, dressing and getting ready for the day, with dignity and patience.

  • Medication support

    Prompting and administering medicines safely by trained, assessed carers.

  • Dementia care

    Familiar faces and steady routines for people living with dementia and their families.

  • Food and cooking

    Planning, preparing and enjoying nutritious meals, together where possible.

  • Shopping and errands

    Help getting to the shops, or doing the shopping for you.

  • Social support

    Company, outings and getting back to the things that make life worth living.

  • Cleaning and home help

    Keeping the house comfortable, tidy and safe.

  • House and garden

    Small jobs around the home and garden that have become harder to manage.

  • Carer support

    Respite and practical help for family carers who need a break.

The co-operative difference

Care that answers to the community, not shareholders

Colne Valley Equitable Care Society Ltd, trading as Co-operative Care Colne Valley, is a multi-stakeholder co-operative. The people we support, our care team and local residents are all members, and our board is elected from that membership.

That means decisions are made locally, any surplus is reinvested in care, and carers are paid fairly. Happier, settled carers mean the same familiar faces at your door.
